How to Play

Clear the board by removing matching pairs of free tiles.

  • A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it, even partly, and it can slide out to the left or the right.
  • Select a free tile, then a matching free tile. Tiles match when they show the same suit and number, the same mountain or the same dragon.
  • Any Flower matches any Flower, and any Season matches any Season.
  • Stuck? Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles into a new order that can still be solved.
  • Undo and Redo are unlimited. Hint shows a pair that keeps the board winnable.

Reading the tiles

Every tile has an index in the top-left corner and a shape under it, so you never need to rely on color:

  • ● circle: Springs 1–9 (count the steaming pools)
  • ■ square: Bathhouses 1–9 (Bathhouse Row from north to south)
  • ◆ diamond: Lakes & Fish 1–9 (count the fish)
  • ▲ triangle: Mountains H, N, W, S (Hot Springs, North, West, Sugarloaf)
  • ⬢ hexagon: Dragons R Red Tile, G Green Tile, ▭ White Stone
  • ✿ flower: Flowers 1–4   ✦ star: Seasons 1–4

Scoring

+10 per pair, plus a combo bonus for quick pairs (within 4 seconds, up to +25). Clearing all 36 Bathhouse tiles earns +100. Hints cost 15, shuffles 50. Winning adds 500 plus 1 point per second under ten minutes.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: click or tap tiles. On small screens, Zoom (or spreading two fingers) enlarges the board; drag to look around, and pinch to zoom back out.
  • Keyboard: ←↑→↓ move, Enter or Space select, H hint, S shuffle, U undo, R redo, F free-tile highlight, Z zoom, P pause, Esc menu.

Tile backs are inspired by the Quapaw Bathhouse's chevron-tiled dome; the real tile colors are not documented, so these are guesses. Facts come from the Hot Springs Games research corpus.
